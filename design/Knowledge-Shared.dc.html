Thyroid tablet on an empty stomach

Content
Levothyroxine works best on an empty stomach. Take it with a glass of water as soon as you wake up, 30 to 60 minutes before breakfast or tea. Keep calcium, iron and antacid tablets at least 4 hours apart from it. If you miss a dose, take it the same day when you remember; do not take two doses the next morning.
